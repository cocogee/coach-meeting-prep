Coach Meeting Prep

Fill this in before each meeting with your coach. It builds a one-page agenda you can save as a PDF or paste into an email. Send it a day or two ahead so your coach can come ready. Your answers stay in this browser and are saved as you type.

The meeting

1Since our last meeting

What happened, good and bad. Keep each item to a line or two.

Wins

Progress, results, things that went better than expected.

    Setbacks

    Things that didn't work, stalled, or surprised you.

      2Where we want your help

      Sort each item by what you need your coach to do.

      Work through together

      One problem to solve live in the meeting. This gets most of the time.

      Nothing to work through this time? Pick a question from the weekly questions channel in Teams and discuss how it applies to your venture.

      Quick questions

      Things your coach can answer in a minute or two.

        Asks

        Things your coach would do after the meeting: an introduction, a resource, feedback on an assignment.

          3Next steps

          What you'll do before the next meeting. Draft these now and confirm them with your coach at the end.

            Your agenda
            This clears this meeting's answers and keeps your team, coach and attendees. Save or copy the agenda first if you need it.
